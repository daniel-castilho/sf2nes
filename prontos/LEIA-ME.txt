ROMs ready to play, built from this project's source (make prontos).
All start from the original demo; byte difference in parentheses.

sf2ne_reset_corrigido.nes     only the soft reset freeze fix (42 bytes).
                              In normal play it's identical to the original (screens, sound and result verified on emulator).

sf2ne_versus.nes              reset fix + VERSUS MODE: the second fighter is controlled by controller 2 (43 bytes).
                              No CPU opponent in this variant. Selection still only offers Ryu.

sf2ne_golpe_forte_unico.nes   reset fix + experimental button adjustment: holding A/B fires the strong hit
                              just once, instead of repeating for up to 14 frames (46 bytes). Changes gameplay.

sf2ne_golpe_na_borda.nes      reset fix + button adjustment (177 bytes): the weak hit comes out on the frame when
                              the button is pressed (rising edge) and, if the button remains held, the weak becomes
                              strong on the 7th frame (promotion). Release before 7 does not repeat the hit. Changes gameplay.

Emulators recommended by the demo's author: Mesen or FCEUX (mapper 5 / MMC5).
Details of each change: docs/BUGS.md.   To rebuild: make prontos   (needs cc65).
